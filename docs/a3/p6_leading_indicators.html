Project 6 A3

Leading indicators

Custom sheet-metal fabrication job shop, about 120 employees, one plant. Weekly series from January 30, 2023 to December 22, 2025 (151 weeks); job-level figures for 2025, whole year and Q2 to Q4.
Sources: ERP, shop-floor data collection, purchasing and kit-check exports (batch 20261003T152921Z-20250101). On-time delivery by ship week; brake backlog in working days of crewed brake capacity.

Background and problem

On-time delivery was 76.6% in 2025 (90.1% in Q2 to Q4) and declined by more than 5 points four times in 36 months.
The largest decline, from the week of December 9, 2024, took the four-week figure from 90.4% to 27.1%. Of the six measures in the review set, one leads on-time delivery.

Target

Every decline in on-time delivery preceded by a flagged indicator at least four weeks earlier.

Analysis

Correlation with on-time delivery by weeks of lead, event weeks excluded; the band is the shuffled 95th percentile.
  • In ordinary weeks only the on-time start rate leads: +0.62 at 1 week against +0.24 and +0.21 by chance.
  • Jobs waiting at the lasers led the 2024 year-end build by 7 weeks (+0.80); the brake series and overtime move with on-time delivery.
  • The on-time start rate moved before 4 of 4 declines against 1.7 by chance; no other series is distinguishable from chance on four declines.
  • Lines promised inside the standard lead time are 19.1% of jobs and 57.9% of late jobs in Q2 to Q4; a short kit carries 85.5% of material lost days.

Expected results

On the record of 2023 to 2025: the on-time start rate moved before 4 of 4 declines, 2 of them four weeks or more ahead, with 6 episodes and 3 followed by a decline; jobs waiting at the lasers before 3 of 4, with 5 episodes and 3 followed. One of the two weekly indicators moved 5, 6, 4 and 7 weeks before the four declines, against 2.7 declines expected on shuffled series and 2.8 on shifted (share at or above 0.21 and 0.19); the pair is not distinguishable from chance. Brake backlog above 3 days marks 4.2% of jobs in Q2 to Q4.

Follow-up

The signal and decline record reviewed quarterly: episodes, declines preceded, and the lead in weeks.
