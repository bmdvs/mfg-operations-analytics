Project 8 A3

Technology ROI

Custom sheet-metal fabrication job shop, about 120 employees, one plant. Three capital options and the no-capital package on the shop model, jobs shipped in 2025, whole year and Q2 to Q4.
Sources: ERP, shop-floor data collection, maintenance exports (batch 20261003T152921Z-20250101); costs and rates supplied by the shop; 30 replications per scenario, 95% intervals. Payback and NPV are expected values under the stated assumptions.

Background and problem

The no-capital package takes on-time delivery to 88.0% for the year against the 95% target.
Three capital options are before the shop: a press brake with automatic tool changing ($735,000), a second automated laser tower ($460,000) and a robotic bending cell ($1,030,000).

Target

95% on time in every quarter.

Analysis

NPV over 7 years against the share of released brake hours sold.
  • On overtime and labor alone no option pays back within 7 years; the package is net negative by $70,000 a year.
  • NPV reaches zero at 15% of released brake hours sold for the cell, 40% for the tool changer on B3 and 24% for the package; the tower releases none.
  • The cell raises on-time delivery 8.1 points for the year, the tool changer 3.9, the package 6.0; every capital option adds under a point in Q2 to Q4.
  • The tower cuts the November 2024 laser queue from 64 to 34 jobs and leaves those jobs' delivery at 73.1%.

Expected results

Expected from the model: the robotic cell on the package raises on-time delivery by 9.5 points (8.6 to 10.4) to 91.5% for the year; its NPV reaches zero when 324 released brake hours a year are sold (16%). No option reaches the target on the promises as made; quoted lead times are P7.
