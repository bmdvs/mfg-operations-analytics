Project 3 A3

The constraint, utilization and variability

Custom sheet-metal fabrication job shop, about 120 employees, one plant. Operations started in 2025, whole year and Q2 to Q4; records from January 2023 to December 2025.
Sources: ERP, shop-floor data collection, maintenance and attendance exports (batch 20261003T152921Z-20250101). Queue in working days per operation, scheduled Saturdays counted.

Background and problem

The brakes run at 0.91 of scheduled hours with a queue of 3.5 days per operation and a 90th percentile of 11.3 days (1.8 and 4.8 in Q2 to Q4).
Machine uptime is 0.96 to 0.98 at every work center and does not show where the load is.

Current condition

Weekly brake queue against weekly utilization, 2023 to 2025, with the curve fitted on 13-week windows.

Target

Brake queue 90th percentile under 5 days in every quarter, the first included. Met in Q3 and Q4 2025 (2.8 and 3.0 days), not in Q1 and Q2 (17.1 and 9.1).

Analysis

Robotic weld cell weekly utilization and queue per operation, 2025.
  • B1 and B2 carry precision and expedited work with the shortest queues; B3 to B5 carry the tail (90th percentile 11.6 to 12.7 days).
  • On the fitted curve the brake queue is 1.51 days at 0.85 utilization, 2.61 at 0.90 and 3.44 at 0.92.
  • In Q2 to Q4 the robotic weld cell has the longest queue: 3.9 days on one shift at 0.83, at or above 0.95 in 17 of 39 weeks.
  • The powder line runs at 0.56 and holds jobs 1.03 days for the color day.
  • Halving the spread of brake setup time releases 0.8 hours a week; the variability is in arrivals and run time.

Results

Measured baseline for 2025: brake utilization 0.91, queue 3.5 days per operation, 90th percentile 11.3; robotic weld queue 3.6 days at 0.83. Results of the countermeasures are reported in P4 and P5.
