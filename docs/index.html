Flow and On-time DeliveryWhere does the lead time go, and does the floor's WIP match the quoted lead times? What makes jobs late, and do the shop's late-reason codes say so? What lead time should be quoted in a build, and which measures warn of a decline?Queue at the brakes is 24% of lead time and the stage that separates late jobs from on-time jobs (5.76 days against 2.50); the median job ships in 11.2 working days against an average quoted lead time of 12.9, 9.2 in Q2-Q4. In 2025, jobs waiting at work centers (i.e., queue constraints) accounted for 42% of the 3,473 lost days, while jobs that were released late accounted for 21%, and jobs that waited on material and outside processing accounted for 7% and 3%, respectively. The shop's quoted lead time was off by 4.8 days on average from 2023 to 2025. The current practice of quoting standard lead times based on the part's routing class, and then adjusting this higher during periods of low capacity, proved to be inaccurate in both directions.Report
Capacity, Constraints and SetupsWhich work centers run hot, and how do their queues respond to load? What do setups cost at the brakes, and where is the overrun?In 2025, the press brakes ran at 91% average utilization and the robotic weld cell at 82%. Against their 97% uptime, the average production capacity buffer was only 6 points for the press brakes and 15 points for the robotic weld cell, the smallest of all work centers. Brake setups ran 1,847 hours over standard in 2025, 36 a week, 10.4 points of brake utilization.Report
Discrete Event Simulation: On-time DeliveryDo release and dispatch rules raise on-time delivery, or only added brake hours? What do the changes that work cost and return?Scenarios that added brake capacity improved on-time delivery. Among the release and dispatch rules, only earliest due date improved on-time delivery. The "Operating Package" combines the brake setup program and additional Saturday shifts on both brakes, the two low cost capacity scenarios. This combination added 7.7 points to on-time delivery. The operating package is recommended first: it raises on-time delivery 7.7 points; the $60,000 initial investment and $15,400 a year pay back in 0.4 years and return $0.69M. The robotic bending cell is the recommended capital investment option: on top of the package it adds 2.2 points and returns $1.52M with a 2.0-year payback.Report
